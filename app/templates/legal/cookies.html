Cookie Policy

Effective date: July 2026 · splanly by SkilledCA Enterprises

This Cookie Policy explains how splanly, operated by SkilledCA Enterprises, uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our platform. It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control their use.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, improve user experience, and provide information to the site owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until deleted or expired) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser).

2. Cookies We Use

splanly uses a minimal set of cookies. We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. Below is a detailed breakdown:

Essential Cookies Required

These cookies are necessary for splanly to function correctly. They cannot be disabled.

Cookie NamePurposeDuration
session_id Maintains your authenticated session. Without this cookie, you would need to log in on every page. Session (expires on logout or browser close)
csrf_token Protects against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks by validating that form submissions originate from splanly. Session

Functional Cookies Optional

These cookies remember your preferences to provide a personalized experience.

Cookie / Storage KeyPurposeDuration
splanly-theme (localStorage) Stores your dark/light theme preference so it persists across visits. Persistent (until cleared by user)
splanly-locale Remembers your language or locale preference (if applicable). Persistent (1 year)

Analytics Cookies Optional

These cookies help us understand how splanly is used so we can improve the platform.

Cookie NamePurposeDuration
_analytics_id Assigns an anonymized identifier to track aggregated usage patterns (page views, feature usage). No personal data is collected through this cookie. 24 months

We may use lightweight, privacy-focused analytics tools. We do not use Google Analytics or any tool that shares your data with third-party advertising networks.

3. Third-Party Cookies

splanly does not intentionally set third-party cookies. However, certain embedded services may set their own cookies:

  • Payment Processor — If you make a payment through our third-party payment processor, that service may set cookies on its own domain to facilitate the transaction and detect fraud. These cookies are governed by the payment processor's own privacy and cookie policies.
  • Embedded Fonts — We load Inter and Plus Jakarta Sans from Google Fonts. Google may collect limited usage data (such as the number of requests) as described in Google's Privacy Policy. No cookies are set by Google Fonts.

We do not use social media tracking pixels, advertising networks, or cross-site remarketing cookies.

4. How to Manage Cookies

You have full control over cookies used by splanly:

  • Browser Settings — Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. You can typically find these settings in the "Privacy" or "Security" section of your browser preferences. Note that blocking essential cookies may prevent splanly from functioning properly.
  • Theme Preference — The splanly-theme setting is stored in localStorage, not as a cookie. You can clear it through your browser's developer tools or by toggling the theme in the application.
  • Analytics Opt-Out — If we offer an in-app toggle for analytics, you can opt out through your account settings. Alternatively, you can block the analytics cookie through your browser.

Browser-specific cookie management guides:

Google Chrome · Mozilla Firefox · Safari · Microsoft Edge

5. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting. splanly respects DNT signals — if your browser sends a DNT signal, we will not set analytics cookies on your visit.
